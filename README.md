### ✦ About Me

I build **digital employees for recruiting**. At [shenlan-ai](https://github.com/shenlan-ai), I'm working on **AIHR**—an AI-powered hiring platform that helps teams screen candidates, manage follow-up conversations, and send interview invitations.

My focus is on Java and Go backends, real-time systems, and making LLM-powered workflows reliable in practice. Outside of AIHR, I build side projects and share [Luogu and LeetCode solutions](https://github.com/shutu-hub/Luogu-and-LeetCode-algorithm-solutions).
